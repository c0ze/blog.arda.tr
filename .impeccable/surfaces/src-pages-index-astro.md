## Scope & mode

`blog.arda.tr` index, post, archive, search and 404. Visitor mode: **Read** —
comprehension and wayfinding first, then make the reading worth staying in.

## Audience, job, action

Peers arriving from GitHub, Mastodon, Bluesky, `arda.tr` or RSS to read one
specific thing. Success is finishing a post and coming back. No funnel.

## Direction contract

**THESIS.** The family system Kara Duman ("his surname means black smoke"),
as applied to the blog: bone paper, one 720px reading column, every post a
numbered row. Adopted 2026-11-23 (`../DESIGN-SYSTEM.md`, slide 13); it
replaced The Index and, before it, One Bit Forest.

**OWN-WORLD.** Soot, bone, ash, ember and moss, from the Astryx `arda` theme's
tokens; light unless the visitor pins dark. Ember for links, numerals and the
label that opens a list; moss for a post's tags. Grenze Gotisch for page and
post titles (Spectral for a long or a Japanese one), Spectral for text,
JetBrains Mono for dates and code. 2px corners, no shadow, no gradient, 1px
rules. One wisp on a soot plate beside a post; no hero images. See DESIGN.md.

**FORM.** Bar (emblem, arda.tr wordmark, `blog`, nav, scheme switch) → title
and lede → an ember label and the row list → tags as chips → footer. The
patterns are brand.css's; don't invent a second vocabulary beside them.
